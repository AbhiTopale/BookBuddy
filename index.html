Library Rules

Please review the official college library regulations and borrowing terms below.

1. Borrowing Period & Limits

• Students may borrow up to 3 books simultaneously.
• Standard borrowing duration for undergraduate students is 14 days.
• Reference books and rare journals must remain inside the reading hall.

2. Book Renewal Policy

• Books can be renewed once for an additional 7 days if no reserve requests exist.
• Renewal can be initiated online directly through the BookBuddy mobile app or at the circulation desk.

3. Late Return & Fine Policy

• Overdue books incur a fine of ₹5 per day per book.
• Accounts with overdue fines exceeding ₹100 will have borrowing privileges temporarily suspended until dues are cleared.

4. Book Damage & Loss

• Markings, highlighting, or tearing pages is strictly prohibited.
• In case of lost books, the borrower must replace the book with the latest edition or pay twice the current market cost.

5. Library Timings & Conduct

• Working Hours: Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM.
• Maintain silence in reading zones.
• Food and open beverages are strictly prohibited inside the main reading room.
